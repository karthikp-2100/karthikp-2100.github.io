I'm a high school senior at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy (IMSA) in Aurora, Illinois. I'm pretty set on a career researching mathematics or physics (probably theoretical), but this is subject to change. I am a **[Regeneron Science Talent Search Scholar](https://www.societyforscience.org/regeneron-sts/)** and a proud alumni of the **[Ross Program](https://rossprogram.org/)**. I encourage any high schooler in math to apply to the Ross Program, as I credit much of my mathematical development to my experiences there. 

My various research projects can be seen **[here](/_research/)**. Blogging can be found on **[Substack](https://substack.com/@karthikprasad2)**

This site is meant to be a collection of my teaching, research, and academic interests. Over time, I intend to add notes from classes I take (and classes I teach), research preprints, talks, book talks, blog posts, and more. Feel free to contact me via email at <karthikp2100@gmail.com> or <kprasad@imsa.edu> for more information.
