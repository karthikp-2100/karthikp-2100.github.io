I am implementing statistics (specifically, implementing hadronic and leptonic taus) for a search for Doubly-Charged Higgs Bosons at CERN. Contributing to the **[IMSA-CMS](https://github.com/IMSA-CMS)** search for doubly-charged Higgs bosons at the Compact Muon Solenoid. Prior work from the group has been published internally by CMS **[here](http://cds.cern.ch/record/2808604?ln=en)**. I have primarily worked with C++, ROOT, and the Combine package. I have used Linux terminal, Github, and remote Fermilab servers in the course of this project.
